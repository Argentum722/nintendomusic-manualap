## Nintendo Music - Manual for Archipelago
A Manual Archipelago Randomizer implementation for the Nintendo Music streaming service

## Requirements:
- An active Nintendo Switch Online subscription
- A device capable of accessing either the Nintendo Music mobile app or the [Nintendo Music webpage](https://music.nintendo.com) (You probably already have this if you're capable of playing Archipelago)

## How this Manual works:
The goal of this Manual is to listen to the soundtracks available on Nintendo Music. Unlock more soundtracks, characters and items by listening to songs.

Once you've listened to all available songs in a soundtrack, you can then mark it as complete. Complete a configurable number of soundtracks to win!

## What is randomized:
- Soundtracks
  - By default, only the "Top Tracks" playlists are used for the Randomizer
- Characters
- Items

## What Soundtracks are available:
As of the current version, the following soundtracks are supported:
- Super Mario Bros. Wonder
- Super Mario Bros. Wonder - Meetup in Bellabel Park*
- Pokémon Scarlet and Pokémon Violet
- Pokémon Scarlet and Pokémon Violet - The Hidden Treasure of Area Zero*
- Mario Kart 8 Deluxe
- Mario Kart 8 Deluxe - Booster Course Pass*
- The Legend of Zelda - Ocarina of Time
- Super Mario World 2 - Yoshi's Island
- Donkey Kong Country
- Kirby's Dream Land
- Dr. Mario (Game Boy)
- Metroid

*DLC Soundtracks, disabled by default

## DISCLAIMER:
As of writing this on November 1st, 2026, there are a total of 11,055 music tracks available on Nintendo Music, with 5,230 of them available in the Top Tracks playlists.
Because of this, playing this Manual in syncs is **NOT** recommended.
